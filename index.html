A clear sequence for a fast-moving emergency.

Household: two adults and one 2.5-year-old, no car. Use the plan in order. During an active attack, start at Step 1 and do not skip ahead to routes while immediate danger is active.

Active attack: start herePreparing in advance

Core rules

The plan only works if these remain true.

  • Shelter during an active attack. Do not evacuate simply because an attack has started.
  • Move only after the immediate danger has passed and authorities permit movement.
  • Treat the first move as a controlled 20–30 km hop, not an attempt to reach Belgium in one push.
  • Never depend on one road, bridge, station, airport, port or destination.
  • Travel in daylight whenever possible.
1

Opening attack: roughly 0–6 hours

Shelter first. Use working utilities while you still have them.

Stay home or in the safest available shelter

Unless Dutch authorities specifically order evacuation.

  • Move away from windows; close doors, windows and ventilation when instructed.
  • Monitor NL-Alert and official Dutch emergency information.
  • Charge phones, powerbanks and bike batteries while power is available.
  • Fill clean water containers while tap water remains declared safe.
  • Keep passports, residence cards, cash, medicines and emergency bags together.
  • Download offline maps and keep both bikes ready.
2

Go / no-go decision

All four GO conditions should be true before using the planned routes.

SHELTER / WAIT — do not use the planned movement routes yet.
Stay if any of these are true

Attacks are still active nearby; NL-Alert says shelter; the route or destination is unknown; or moving requires an unverified damaged or congested chokepoint.

3

First movement: 20–30 km

The first destination is a checkpoint, not the final evacuation destination.

Primary corridor

Preferred
Haarlem → Bennebroek → Hillegom → Lisse

Hillegom is the minimum useful displacement point. Lisse is the preferred first checkpoint. Plan roughly 2–4 hours by bicycle with a toddler, bags, stops and disruption.

Backup corridor

If primary fails
Haarlem → Vogelenzang → De Zilk → Noordwijkerhout

Use this if the southern corridor is not viable. Stop at Noordwijkerhout and reassess rather than automatically continuing down the coast.

4

First checkpoint: stop and reassess

At Lisse or Noordwijkerhout, do not automatically keep moving.

5

Second movement

Continue only if conditions remain favorable.

  • Make another controlled 20–40 km hop later that day or the next morning.
  • Possible staging direction: Voorhout / Sassenheim area, then reassess again.
  • Do not pre-commit to Leiden, Rotterdam, Belgium or a specific bridge or tunnel. Choose the next segment from verified conditions at that time.
6

Long-distance exit

Use surviving infrastructure. Do not depend on it.

If sustained NATO–Russia warfare develops, the objective becomes leaving the active theater while civilian transport still functions.

What the bikes are for

They are not meant to take the family all the way to Belgium. They bridge gaps between surviving pieces of transport infrastructure.

!

Areas to deprioritize

Unless authorities direct otherwise.

  • Amsterdam and Schiphol
  • IJmuiden and major port / industrial infrastructure
  • Katwijk / Valkenburg area
  • Wassenaar and major Defence concentrations
  • The Hague government / Defence district
  • Rotterdam port and major logistics infrastructure
  • Large rail junctions
  • Strategic bridges / tunnels unless confirmed usable and necessary

These are planning exclusions, not predictions of attack.

Decision tree

The entire plan in one sequence.

1
Active attack?

Yes → shelter. Do not move into the transport network.

2
Attack stopped and authorities permit movement?

No → continue sheltering. Yes → assess the wider situation.

3
Conflict escalating + viable daylight route?

Yes → Primary: Lisse. Backup: Noordwijkerhout.

4
Stop and reassess.

No safe onward route → shelter locally. Safe route → another 20–40 km controlled hop.

5
Reach functioning long-distance transport.

Leave the affected region → leave continental Europe if necessary → USA.
